# RepTrack assets

All exercise SVG illustrations and application icons were created specifically for RepTrack. No external stock images, copyrighted photos, fonts, or runtime image services are used. The project may use, modify, and redistribute these original assets without third-party attribution requirements.

The diagrams are simplified movement references: lime limbs show a working position, dashed limbs indicate another position, arrows indicate the direction of movement, and muted gray depicts equipment. They are not personalized coaching.

The source designation “Incline Dumbbell Press (bar)” is ambiguous. Its initial illustration uses a barbell on an incline bench; the exercise name and recorded weight must remain unchanged until the user confirms the equipment. The application should expose the equipment uncertainty and allow image replacement.

The calf press illustration depicts a calf press on a leg press machine. Machine geometry differs by gym. The rear delt image shows a reverse pec deck, and hip adduction is shown from the front.

Files are bundled locally and should be included in the PWA cache. Each exercise SVG contains an accessible title and description.
